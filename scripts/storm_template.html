Tap any pipe to follow the water: the route to its outfall is drawn in orange, and the pipes that feed it stay lit. Each color patch is one outfall's drainage area. Pipes 4 ft and larger get a dark outline. Storm drains carry untreated rainwater straight to streams and ponds. Routes follow the town's downstream pipe links, with pipe geometry used where links are missing; the receiving stream or pond is the nearest one on the town's water map, and many small brooks and wetlands aren't mapped. Street names are the nearest street to each pipe. Snapshot of Town of Manchester GIS (UtilitySystem/Storm, water bodies, StreetName), pulled September 23, 2026.
